[b][size=5]Crop Optimizer[/size][/b] [i](v1.4.8)[/i]

Optimize your farm day with crop forecasting and a compact HUD.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Field-level harvest forecasting with projected sell-value totals
[*]Lightweight HUD with drag positioning and configurable scale
[*]Growth and crop-state tracking with practical, in-session visibility
[*]Soft integrations with Sun Haven Todo, Birthday Reminder, The Vault, and Haven's Almanac
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx]BepInEx 5.x[/url] for Sun Haven
[*]Sun Haven
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Place [b]CropOptimizer.dll[/b] in [b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/plugins/CropOptimizer/[/b]
[*]Launch the game
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/config/CropOptimizer.cfg[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Enabled[/b] - Master toggle
[*][b]HUD.Enabled[/b], [b]HUD.Scale[/b], [b]HUD.PositionX[/b], [b]HUD.PositionY[/b], [b]HUD.ToggleKey[/b]
[*][b]Debug.DebugLogging[/b] for troubleshooting output
[/list]
