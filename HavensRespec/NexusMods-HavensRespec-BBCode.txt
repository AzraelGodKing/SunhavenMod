[b][size=5]Haven's Respec[/size][/b] [i](v1.3.7)[/i]

Reset Sun Haven skill trees safely with confirmation, undo, and configurable costs.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Styled Reset button on every profession tab
[*]Confirmation dialog with point refund and cost preview
[*]Per-profession undo history in the same play session
[*]Configurable cost model: None, Gold, or Gems
[*]Optional Reset All flow plus optional hotkeys for reset/undo
[*]Configuration Manager-compatible settings for live in-game tuning
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx]BepInEx 5.x[/url] for Sun Haven
[*]Sun Haven
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Place [b]HavensRespec.dll[/b] in [b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/plugins/HavensRespec/[/b]
[*]Launch the game
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/config/HavensRespec.cfg[/b]

[list]
[*][b]General.Enabled[/b] - Master toggle
[*][b]UI[/b] settings for button injection, confirmation, undo, and reset-all
[*][b]Cost[/b] settings for mode and per-point values
[*][b]Hotkeys[/b] settings for quick reset/undo actions
[/list]
