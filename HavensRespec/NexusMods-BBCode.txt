[B]Haven's Respec[/B]

Respec your Sun Haven skill trees safely. A styled Reset button on every skill tab, with confirmation, undo, configurable cost, and full BepInEx Configuration Manager support.

[*]Clean-room reset that walks the live skill tree (no hardcoded node names) — survives future Sun Haven updates.
[*]Confirmation dialog with per-profession breakdown and cost preview.
[*]One-step Undo per profession (session-only).
[*]Cost model: None / Gold / Gems with configurable cost per refunded point.
[*]Optional Shift-click to skip confirmation, plus optional hotkeys for reset and undo.
[*]Full Configuration Manager visibility (config lives in BepInEx/config/HavensRespec.cfg).
[/LIST]
