[b][size=5]Haven's Birthright[/size][/b] [i](v2.1.0)[/i]

A Sun Haven mod that adds unique racial bonuses, active abilities, drawbacks, and conditional synergies for each playable race.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Thematic [b]passive[/b] bonuses per race; select races get powerful [b]active[/b] abilities (toggle in-game where noted)
[*][b]Drawbacks[/b] — optional thematic penalties ([b]disabled by default[/b])
[*][b]Conditional synergies[/b] — time of day, season, HP threshold
[*]Almost all values are [b]configurable[/b]
[*][b]F12[/b] (default) — reload config from disk without restarting
[/list]

Active abilities [b]Infernal Forge[/b], [b]Tidal Blessing[/b], [b]Font of Light[/b], and [b]Soul Harvest[/b] use the in-game toggle: they [b]start OFF[/b] each session until you press [b]F9[/b] (configurable); you can press [b]F9[/b] whenever you like to turn them [b]ON[/b] or [b]OFF[/b] again. Others may be config-only.

[spoiler][size=3][b]Passive racial bonuses (summary)[/b][/size]
[b]Human:[/b] Adaptable (+XP), Charismatic (+relationship), Silver Tongue (shop discount)

[b]Elf:[/b] Nature's Touch (farming), Green Thumb (crop quality), Forest Walker (foraging), Arcane Heritage (mana regen)

[b]Angel:[/b] Divine Reservoir (max mana), Holy Light (magic damage), Blessed Recovery (HP regen), Fortune's Favor (luck)

[b]Demon:[/b] Infernal Might (melee), Ruthless (crit), Hellforged Vitality (max HP), Greed (gold drops)

[b]Fire Elemental:[/b] Burning Fury, Inferno, Wildfire, Scorching Strike (melee/magic/attack speed/crit)

[b]Water Elemental:[/b] Tidal Shield (defense), Healing Waters, Flowing Spirit, Aquatic Kinship (fishing)

[b]Amari:[/b] Swift Paws (move speed), Predator's Reflexes (attack speed), Skilled Artisan (crafting), Forest Hunter (woodcutting)

[b]Amari Cat:[/b] Feline Grace (move speed), Predator's Strike (crit), Keen Senses (foraging), [b]Nine Lives[/b] (passive cheat-death chance — configurable, max 60%)

[b]Naga:[/b] Aquatic Nature (fishing speed), Sea's Blessing (fishing luck), Scaled Hide (defense), Tidal Magic (mana regen)
[/spoiler]

[spoiler][size=3][b]Active abilities (detail)[/b][/size]
[b]Water Elemental — Tidal Blessing:[/b] Auto-waters hoed crop tiles near the player at HP cost. Configurable HP%, safety threshold, cooldown.

[b]Fire Elemental — Infernal Forge:[/b] Periodically smelts ore → bars using mana; tiered mana costs by ore type; blocks all mana regen while active; safety threshold.

[b]Angel — Font of Light (Celestial Bloodlines):[/b] Restores mana over time at gold cost. Angel race only. [b]F9[/b] toggle.

[b]Demon — Soul Harvest (Celestial Bloodlines):[/b] Bonus gold on kill, small HP cost per kill. Demon race only. [b]F9[/b] toggle.
[/spoiler]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/config/com.azraelgodking.havensbirthright.cfg[/b]

You can enable/disable bonuses globally; toggle actives, drawbacks, and synergies; adjust percentages; set [b]F9[/b] / [b]F12[/b]; tune HP/mana costs and thresholds; [b]Amari Cat[/b] — Nine Lives under [b][Amari Cat][/b]; [b]AmariCatReduceCombatStutter[/b] under [b][Performance][/b] if needed.

[spoiler][size=3][b]Bonus transfers (optional)[/b][/size]
Section [b][BonusTransfers][/b]: [b]EnableBonusTransfers[/b] defaults [b]false[/b]. When true, [b]Rules[/b] are semicolon-separated [b]TargetRace|SourceRace|BonusType[/b] — extra [b]passive[/b] percentages for the target, using the source race’s configured value for that bonus type. Does not copy actives, drawbacks, or synergies.
[/spoiler]

[spoiler][size=3][b]Changelog (recent)[/b][/size]
[b]v2.0.0[/b] — Changing saves or characters—and Fire/Water Elemental behavior after loads and F9—is more reliable. The code was reorganized for upkeep, and the mod keeps its hidden runner alive with shared SceneRootSurvivor instead of a third-party Keep Alive mod.

[b]v1.4.3[/b] — Optional [b][BonusTransfers][/b] cross-race passive grants ([b]EnableBonusTransfers[/b] off by default; [b]Rules[/b] [b]Target|Source|BonusType[/b]). CheatEnabler compatibility: mod hotkeys defer while typing in chat, console, or UI text fields.

[b]v1.4.0[/b] — Save-load fix: race detection and ability state reset correctly when switching saves without returning to main menu. F12 config reload.

[b]v1.3.x[/b] — Amari Cat rework; Nine Lives passive; optional performance setting for stutter with fast weapons.

[b]v1.2.0[/b] — Tidal Blessing, Infernal Forge, F9 toggle, drawbacks, conditional synergies.

[b]v1.1.0[/b] — Initial passives for all races.
