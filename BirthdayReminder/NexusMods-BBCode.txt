[b][size=5]A Squirrel's Birthday Reminder[/size][/b] [i](v1.3.0)[/i]

Never miss an NPC birthday in Sun Haven. Auto reminders, gift suggestions, gift tracking, draggable HUD.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Auto Reminders[/b] — Notified when NPCs have birthdays
[*][b]Gift Suggestions[/b] — Loved and liked items per NPC
[*][b]Gift Tracking[/b] — Track who you've already gifted today
[*][b]Draggable HUD[/b] — Position the reminder anywhere on screen
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Usage[/b][/size]
The mod automatically displays birthday reminders when you load into the game on an NPC's birthday.

[line]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx]BepInEx 5.x[/url]
[*]Sun Haven
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Place [b]BirthdayReminder.dll[/b] in [b]BepInEx/plugins/[/b] (subfolder OK)
[*]Launch the game
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[b]BepInEx/config/com.azraelgodking.squirrelsbirthdayreminder.cfg[/b]
