Sun Haven TodoIn-Game Task Tracker

Never Forget Your Farm Tasks Again

v1.1.7
~ A Record of Things To Do ~

Dear Journal, Sun Haven Todo is an in-game task tracker that helps you organize and remember all your farming goals, quests, and daily activities. With a beautiful parchment-themed UI that matches Sun Haven's aesthetic, categories for different activities, priority levels, and per-character saves, you'll stay on top of everything your farm life demands.

How to Open

Ctrl + T
Open Todo List
Esc
Close Todo List

✨ A Note About Gameplay

When you open the Todo List, player input is disabled so you won't accidentally move your character while managing tasks. Press Escape or close the window to resume playing!

🔗 Enchanted Quill (Auto-Todos)

Other mods can automatically create and complete todos for you:

  • Birthday Reminder — Creates "Give [NPC] a birthday gift!" todos each morning, auto-completes when you give the gift
  • Senpai's Chest + S.M.U.T. — Creates "Donate [Item] to the museum" todos when undonated items are found in chests, auto-completes when donated

Journal Entries

📋

Task Management

Create, complete, and delete tasks with ease. Add titles and descriptions to keep track of exactly what you need to do.

🏷

Categories

Organize tasks by activity type: Farming, Mining, Fishing, Combat, Crafting, Social, Quests, Collection, or General.

🔥

Priority Levels

Mark tasks as Low, Normal, High, or Urgent priority. High-priority tasks are always shown at the top of your list.

👤

Per-Character Saves

Each character has their own separate todo list. Switch between characters and your tasks are always saved correctly.

🔍

Search & Filter

Search for tasks by name or filter by category. Toggle completed tasks on or off to focus on what's left.

📊

Progress Tracking

See your completion statistics with a visual progress bar. Track how many tasks you've finished vs. how many remain.

🔗

Cross-Mod Integration

Other mods can auto-create and auto-complete todos. Birthday gifts, museum donations, and more — all tracked automatically.

Writing in Your Journal

1

Open Your Journal

Press Ctrl + T to open the Todo List window. The game will pause automatically so you can manage your tasks.

2

Jot Down a Task

Click the + Add button to create a new task. Enter a title, optional description, select a priority level and category.

3

Flip to a Chapter

Use the category tabs to filter your tasks. Click "All" to see everything, or select a specific category like Farming or Combat.

4

Cross It Off

Click the checkbox next to any task to mark it as complete. Completed tasks move to the bottom and are grayed out.

5

Tear Out Old Pages

Use "Clear Done" to remove all completed tasks, or click the X button on individual tasks to delete them.

Getting Your Journal

📦

1. Install BepInEx

Download and install BepInEx 5.x for Unity games if you haven't already.

📥

2. Download Sun Haven Todo

Get SunhavenTodo.dll from the releases page or Nexus Mods.

📁

3. Install the Mod

Place the DLL in your BepInEx/plugins/ folder.

🎮

4. Start Writing!

Launch Sun Haven and press Ctrl+T to start tracking your tasks.
