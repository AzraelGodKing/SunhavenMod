Nexus Mods detailed descriptions (BBCode)
==========================================

Each mod folder has a NexusMods-BBCode.txt file: copy its contents into that mod's Nexus "Detailed Description" field (BBCode mode).

Paths (relative to repo root):

  SenpaisChest/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  SunhavenTodo/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  SunHavenMuseumUtilityTracker/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  TheVault/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  HavensBirthright/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  HavensAlmanac/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  BirthdayReminder/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  HavenDevTools/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  TrinketFortune/NexusMods-BBCode.txt
  FasterRaces/NexusMods-BBCode.txt

Keep these in sync when you edit the matching README.md / thunderstore README.

Those files use Nexus BBCode tags (b, i, url, list, table, spoiler, code, quote, size, line).
