[b][size=5]Faster Races[/size][/b] [i](v1.2.0)[/i]

Adds a [b]configurable movement speed bonus[/b] for your character. When used with [b]Haven's Birthright[/b], Birthright's [b]movement speed bonuses[/b] are disabled so you don't get double speed — one consistent bonus from Faster Races. Speed [b]penalties[/b] from Birthright (e.g. some Amari lines, Naga) still apply.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Configurable speed[/b] — Set the bonus percentage in config (default +25%)
[*][b]Compatible with Haven's Birthright[/b] — If both are installed, Birthright skips its own move-speed buffs (e.g. Amari Cat, Amari Bird Tailwind)
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[b]BepInEx/config/com.azraelgodking.fasterraces.cfg[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Enabled[/b] — Turn the speed bonus on or off (default: true)
[*][b]SpeedBonusPercent[/b] — Percentage added to movement speed (default: 25)
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]

[*]Sun Haven
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[*]Launch the game
[/list]
