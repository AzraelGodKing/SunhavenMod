[b][size=5]Sunhaven Todo[/size][/b] [i](v2.1.1)[/i]

An in-game to-do list for Sun Haven with per-character saves, categories, priorities, and a parchment-styled UI.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]In-Game Todo List[/b] — Add, edit, complete, and delete tasks without leaving the game
[*][b]Per-Character Lists[/b] — Each character has their own separate todo file
[*][b]Categories & Priorities[/b] — Organize tasks by category and urgency
[*][b]Search & Filters[/b] — Search titles/descriptions, filter by category, hide completed items
[*][b]Progress Stats[/b] — Totals and completion percentage at a glance
[*][b]Auto-Save[/b] — Saves on a timer and on game exit
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Install BepInEx 5.x for Sun Haven
[*]Build or download [b]SunhavenTodo.dll[/b]
[*]Copy to [b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/plugins/SunhavenTodo/[/b]
[*]Launch the game
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
After first launch, edit:
[b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/config/com.azraelgodking.sunhaventodo.cfg[/b]

[table]
[tr][th]Setting[/th][th]Default[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]ToggleKey[/td][td]T[/td][td]Key to open/close the todo list[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]RequireCtrl[/td][td]true[/td][td]Require Ctrl with ToggleKey[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]AutoSave[/td][td]true[/td][td]Automatically save the todo list[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]AutoSaveInterval[/td][td]60[/td][td]Auto-save interval (seconds)[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[line]

[size=4][b]Data storage[/b][/size]
Todo lists are saved per character under:
[b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/config/com.azraelgodking.sunhaventodo/[/b]
Files: [b]<CharacterName>_todos.json[/b] (sanitized names).
