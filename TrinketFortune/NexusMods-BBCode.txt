[b][size=5]Trinket Fortune[/size][/b] [i](v1.0.2)[/i]

Increases the odds of [b]unowned fishing trinkets[/b] dropping as you complete the museum aquarium. Less frustrating fishing for that last trinket.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Museum-aware[/b] — As you donate more aquarium trinkets, drop odds for trinkets you don't own yet increase
[*][b]Configurable[/b] — Bonus strength and minimum museum progress threshold
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[b]BepInEx/config/com.azraelgodking.trinketfortune.cfg[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Enabled[/b] — On/off (default: true)
[*][b]MuseumProgressBonusPercent[/b] — Bonus to unowned trinket odds per 10% aquarium completion (default: 5)
[*][b]MinimumMuseumProgress[/b] — No bonus below this fraction (default: 0.2 = 20%)
[/list]

[line]

[/list]
